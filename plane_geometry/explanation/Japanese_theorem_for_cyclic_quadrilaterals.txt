Specifically, let □ABCD be an arbitrary cyclic quadrilateral and let M1, M2, M3, M4 be the incenters of the triangles △ABD, △ABC, △BCD, △ACD. Then the quadrilateral formed by M1, M2, M3, M4 is a rectangle.
