The Fuhrmann triangle, named after Wilhelm Fuhrmann (1833–1904), is special triangle based on a given arbitrary triangle.

For a given triangle ABC and its circumcircle, the midpoints of the arcs over triangle sides are denoted by M_{a},M_{b},M_{c}. Those midpoints get reflected at the associated triangle sides yielding the points M_{a}^{\prime},M_{b}^{\prime},M_{c}^{\prime}, which forms the Fuhrmann triangle.
