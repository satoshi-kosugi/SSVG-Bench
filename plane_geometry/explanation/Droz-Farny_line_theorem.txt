Let T be a triangle with vertices A, B, and C, and let H be its orthocenter (the common point of its three altitude lines). Let L_1 and L_2 be any two mutually perpendicular lines through H. Let A_1, B_1, and C_1 be the points where L_1 intersects the (possibly extended) side lines BC, CA, and AB, respectively. Similarly, let A_2, B_2, and C_2 be the points where L_2 intersects (possibly extended) those side lines. The Droz-Farny line theorem says that the midpoints of the three segments {A_1}{A_2}, {B_1}{B_2}, and {C_1}{C_2} are collinear.
