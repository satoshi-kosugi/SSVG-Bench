In Euclidean geometry, the orthotransversal of a point is the line defined as follows.

For a triangle ABC and a point P, three orthotraces, intersections of lines BC, CA, AB and perpendiculars of AP, BP, CP through P respectively are collinear. The lines BC, CA, AB may need to be extended. The line which includes these three points is called the orthotransversal of P.
