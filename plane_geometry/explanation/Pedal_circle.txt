The pedal circle of a triangle ABC and a point P in the plane is a special circle determined by those two entities. More specifically for the three perpendiculars through the point P onto the three (extended) triangle sides a, b, c you get three points of intersection Pa, Pb, Pc and the circle defined by those three points is the pedal circle.
